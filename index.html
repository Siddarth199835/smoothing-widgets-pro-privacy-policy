Privacy Policy for Smoothing Widgets Pro

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smoothing Widgets Pro ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

Smoothing Widgets Pro is a paid Android application that provides customizable home-screen widgets, including clock, calendar, calculator, battery, and Bluetooth-related widgets.

We respect your privacy and are committed to minimizing the collection and use of personal information.

1. Information We Access

Smoothing Widgets Pro may access certain device information locally when required to provide specific widget functionality.

Depending on the widgets you use, this may include:

  • Battery status and battery-related information.
  • Bluetooth connection and Bluetooth device status information.
  • Date and time information required for clock and calendar widgets.
  • Information required to operate and update home-screen widgets.

This information is used to provide the corresponding functionality requested by the user.

2. Bluetooth Information

Some features of Smoothing Widgets Pro provide Bluetooth-related widgets and functionality.

When you use these features, the App may access Bluetooth connection and device status information necessary to display the status of Bluetooth functionality or connected Bluetooth devices.

Bluetooth information is used only to provide the relevant Bluetooth functionality of the App.

The App does not use Bluetooth information to track your location, monitor your movements, or build a personal profile about you.

3. Battery Information

Battery widgets may access the device's battery status so that information such as battery level or charging status can be displayed on the user's home screen.

Battery information is used only for the operation of the battery widgets and is not used to identify you personally.

4. Clock, Calendar, and Calculator Widgets

The App provides various clock, calendar, and calculator widgets.

Clock widgets use the device's date and time information to display the correct time.

Calendar widgets use date and calendar-related information required to display calendar functionality.

Calculator widgets process calculations locally on the device.

5. Home-Screen Widgets

Smoothing Widgets Pro uses Android's home-screen widget framework to provide and update widgets.

Widget-related information is used to display the features selected by the user on the Android home screen.

The App does not use home-screen widget functionality to collect personal information for advertising or user profiling.

6. Personal Information

Smoothing Widgets Pro does not require users to provide personal information such as their name, postal address, phone number, or government identification in order to use the App's widget features.

The App does not require users to create an account to use its core functionality.

7. Data Collection and Sharing

Smoothing Widgets Pro does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to our servers.

The device information accessed for widget functionality is used locally on the user's device to provide the requested features.

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Smoothing Widgets Pro does not contain third-party advertising services such as AdMob.

8. Paid App and Payments

Smoothing Widgets Pro is distributed as a paid application through Google Play.

Purchases made through Google Play are processed by Google according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Smoothing Widgets Pro does not receive or store your credit card, debit card, bank account, or other payment credentials.

9. Permissions

The App may request Android permissions when they are required for specific functionality.

Bluetooth-related permissions may be required for Bluetooth widgets and Bluetooth functionality.

These permissions are used only for the features for which they are required.

10. Data Security

Because Smoothing Widgets Pro does not intentionally transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a remote database of users' personal information.

Information used by the App for local widget functionality remains on the user's device unless the Android operating system or another service independently handles it.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Smoothing Widgets Pro does not intentionally maintain a server-side collection of users' personal information.

Information used locally by the App remains on the user's device according to the App's local storage and Android's system behavior.

Users can remove locally stored App data by using the Android system's application settings or by uninstalling the App, where applicable.

12. Third-Party Services

Smoothing Widgets Pro does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect personal information.

The App may rely on Android system services and Google Play services necessary for distribution or normal Android functionality.

Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

13. Children's Privacy

Smoothing Widgets Pro is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the App's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page and the effective date will be updated accordingly.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Smoothing Widgets Pro, please contact us.
