Privacy Policy for Smoothing Widgets Pro

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smoothing Widgets Pro ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

Smoothing Widgets Pro is a home-screen widget application that provides widgets such as clocks, calendars, calculators, battery information, and Bluetooth-related widgets.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection and use of personal information.

1. Information We Access

Smoothing Widgets Pro may access certain device information required to provide its widget functionality.

Depending on the widgets you use, this may include:

  • Battery status and battery-related information.
  • Bluetooth connection and device status information.
  • Information required to display clocks, dates, calendars, and calculators.
  • Information required by Android to operate and update home-screen widgets.

The App uses this information to provide the functionality requested by the user, such as displaying battery status or Bluetooth connection status in a home-screen widget.

2. Bluetooth Information

Some features of Smoothing Widgets Pro provide Bluetooth-related widgets and functionality.

When you use these features, the App may access Bluetooth information necessary to determine the status of Bluetooth connections or paired Bluetooth devices.

Bluetooth information is used only to provide the relevant Bluetooth widget functionality.

The App does not use Bluetooth information to track your location or monitor your activities outside the functionality provided by the App.

3. Battery Information

Battery widgets may access the device's battery status so that the current battery information can be displayed on the user's home screen.

Battery information is used for the operation of the battery widgets and is not intended to identify you personally.

4. Home-Screen Widgets

Smoothing Widgets Pro provides Android home-screen widgets. Android may provide the App with information necessary for widgets to operate and update correctly.

Widget functionality may include clocks, calendars, calculators, battery information, and Bluetooth status.

Widget information is used to display the requested content on the user's home screen.

5. Personal Information

Smoothing Widgets Pro does not require you to create an account or provide information such as your name, postal address, or phone number in order to use the core widget functionality.

We do not intentionally request personal information unless it is necessary for a specific feature or support interaction.

6. Data Collection and Sharing

We do not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Device information accessed for widget functionality is used to provide the requested features of the App.

We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties for purposes unrelated to providing, maintaining, securing, or improving the App.

If third-party services are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

7. Permissions

The App may request Android permissions when they are required for particular functionality.

For example, Bluetooth-related permissions may be required for Bluetooth widgets and features.

Permissions are used only for the functionality for which they are requested.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. We do not intentionally collect or retain personal information that is not necessary for the operation of the App.

9. Children's Privacy

Smoothing Widgets Pro is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Third-Party Services

Smoothing Widgets Pro may use third-party services or libraries that are necessary for certain features of the App.

If third-party services that collect or process user information are included in the App, their data practices will be disclosed in this Privacy Policy as applicable.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page and the effective date will be updated accordingly.
